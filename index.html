02 · Paste Stack (⇧⌘C)

Queue multiple copies, then paste in order.

Need to move four fields from a browser into a form or terminal? Press ⇧⌘C to start a FIFO queue. Copy each item once, then press ⌘V repeatedly to paste them in sequence.

  • First-In, First-Out (FIFO) or LIFO ordering
  • Optional plain-text stripping per stack
  • Auto-closes when the queue finishes

03 · Privacy & Pinboards

Secrets stay out. Pinned items stay forever.

Void automatically drops concealed and transient clipboard markers from password managers like 1Password and macOS Passwords, while giving pinned items 100% immunity from retention cleanup.

  • Blocks org.nspasteboard.ConcealedType automatically
  • One-click Pause for 15m, 30m, or 1h (⌘T)
  • Portable .voidboard archive export & import
